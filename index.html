About

Who I am

15+ years in IT — from hands-on engineering to leading complex programs across global industries.

Based in Barcelona, I'm a Telecoms Engineer working as Senior IT Program Manager at dsm-firmenich, a global company operating across nutrition, health and beauty. I started my career in network engineering and transitioned into management in 2015 — since then delivering high-visibility global programs with budget ownership, executive reporting, and cross-functional teams spanning multiple countries and vendors.

My track record covers four industries: chemical, pharma, aeronautical and automotive — each with its own regulatory constraints, stakeholder culture and delivery pace. That breadth shapes how I approach complexity: with structure, adaptability, and a bias for clarity over process. I've also served as TFM Tutor at La Salle University (Barcelona), guiding Master's students through their final projects in Project Management.

I write about practical PM and leadership at pmtricks.net and on Medium. Away from work, I play padel, hit the gym, and read — mostly fantasy and historical fiction (Sanderson, Gómez-Jurado, Molist).
